## ✨ Key Features

**Outfits & Armor Skins**

- 🧥 **Outfits** — browse and apply every costume set in the catalogue, from the Ancient Sorcerer and Gilded sets to collaboration outfits and battle pass rewards. Outfits are purely cosmetic and do not affect gameplay or abilities[reference:0].
- 🎨 **Dye & Pattern Preview** — inspect dye colours and sewing thread patterns applied to any outfit before unlocking them in the Mirror Boutique[reference:1].
- 🛡️ **Armor Slot Skins** — preview individual head, chest, and leg appearances, including illusion-converted armor pieces.
- 🏷️ **Slot-Aware Filtering** — head only works with head, chest with chest, matching the game's illusion slot rules[reference:2].

**Weapons & Morphs**

- 🔫 **Weapon Skins** — browse and apply weapon appearances for every weapon type — greatsword, longsword, dagger, crossbow, longbow, staff, wand, spear, orb, and gauntlets — with type-locked filtering so swords only show sword skins[reference:3].
- 🪽 **Morphs** — preview and apply transformation forms, including flight, dash, and swim morphs such as the Amarian Ferthur Flight Morph and Fluffy Almiraz Dash Morph[reference:4].
- 🎭 **Amitoi** — browse and apply collectible travel companion appearances[reference:5].

**Illusion Workshop Integration**

- 🔮 **Illusion Preview** — preview the appearance of any Epic gear piece before converting it into an illusion with Illusion Scrolls (6 for weapons, 2–3 for armor)[reference:6].
- 🖼️ **Mirror Boutique Browser** — browse the combined appearance collection and mix armor weight classes freely — light, heavy, and medium armor illusions are interchangeable across weight types[reference:7].
- 🎨 **Illusion Dyeing** — preview dye colours on any illusion before committing scrolls to the conversion.

**Interface**

- 🔍 **Searchable Catalogue** — filter by slot, weapon type, set name, or event source.
- ⭐ **Favorites & Loadouts** — save complete per-character appearance loadouts and switch between them instantly.
- 🧭 **Tabbed Menu** — outfits, weapons, morphs, illusions, and settings grouped cleanly.
- 👁️ **Instant Preview** — nothing changes on your character until you confirm the selection.
